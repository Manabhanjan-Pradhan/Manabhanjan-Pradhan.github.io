About Me

Research Scientist formulating cybersecurity solutions for industrial platforms

Cybersecurity Specialist & Research Scientist

I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Rourkela in 2025, under the supervision of Dr. Sujata Mohanty. Currently, I am a Research Scientist at CAMS Pvt Ltd, working on implementing cutting-edge enterprise information security schemes.

My academic and professional journey has been focused on developing lightweight mutual authentication protocols, cryptographic architectures (Elliptic Curve Cryptography), decentralized blockchain trust protocols, and high-precision intrusion detection networks.

Core Competence

During my PhD, I developed lightweight authentication solutions for resource-constrained IoT nodes to prevent computationally heavy operations locally.

At CAMS Pvt Ltd, I am building upon this expertise, extending blockchain-based dynamic consensus routines and machine learning intrusion models to secure medical, vehicular, and cloud networks.
