About Me

Researching sustainable security paradigms for next-generation networks

Doctoral Researcher at NIT Rourkela

Since admitting to the doctoral program at NIT Rourkela in 2018 under the supervision of Dr. Sujata Mohanty, my academic journey has been dedicated to resolving critical security issues in decentralised, fog-assisted IoT and vehicular networks.

My research sits at the intersection of cryptography (Elliptic Curve Cryptography), blockchain technology, and artificial intelligence, aiming to deliver robust mutual authentication protocols and high-performance intrusion detection systems.
